points to remember - Evaluation criteria
1. Business Impact
Clearly explain the value the solution brings to the client.
Demonstrate potential time savings and broader impact.
Show whether the idea can scale beyond an individual team or demo.
The strongest ideas should have enough client value that they could eventually be developed further and pitched to the client.
2. Innovation & Creativity
Clearly define the problem being addressed and the proposed solution.
Explain what makes the idea original or creative.
Teams should consider how AI and potentially multiple tools contribute to the solution.
The deck should explain how the concept could scale beyond the initial hackathon demonstration.
3. Technical Implementation & Responsible AI
Teams should address security, quality, scalability, Responsible AI, and architecture.
The presentation should show that the proposed architecture can support broader enterprise use rather than simply functioning as a local demo.
Teams should explicitly explain how Responsible AI and quality are incorporated into the proposed solution.
4. Presentation
The deck needs to communicate the idea clearly and convincingly.
Teams should make the presentation engaging for judges and articulate both the problem and proposed solution.
The presentation should effectively function as a pitch for why the idea deserves to move forward.
